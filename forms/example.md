### 1. Verbed noun
Form: a noun pressed into service as a verb where a plain verb already exists.
Examples: "let's action that" / "we need to solution this" / "I'll diary it" / "can you architect the deck"
Instead: use the verb. "Let's do that." "Put it in the calendar."

### 2. Alignment euphemism
Form: naming a disagreement, decision, or refusal as a state of agreement in order to avoid saying what happened.
Examples: "we're aligned on next steps" (meaning one side conceded) / "let's take that offline" / "circling back on this"
Instead: say what happened. "Priya wants to ship Friday and I agreed." "I don't have an answer; I'll email you Tuesday."
