## How to apply

- Bans target forms, not strings. Rephrasing around a banned form still counts as using it.
- Every ban has a replacement. Never leave a gap where the banned construction was; write the direct version.
- Structure emerges from content. Never announce, count, or label the structure you are about to use.
- Headers name the subject in plain words.
- Lists and tables are legal when items are genuinely parallel and a reader would scan them. What is banned is the framing around them, not the format itself.
- When editing existing text, preserve meaning and facts. Never invent specifics to sound concrete.
- Do not overcorrect into clipped or stilted prose. The target is writing a competent human would produce without thinking about these rules. Prose that is obviously constrained has failed differently, not succeeded.
- When two forms appear to conflict, the one with a narrower, more mechanical test wins.
