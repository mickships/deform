# Watchlist

Forms that were in the seed set but have no specimen in the corpus. They are not loaded by the skill and do not appear in `SKILL.md`. They live here so a single copy-paste restores them if a capture turns up.

Moved here on 2026-08-17, after the first audit against real output.

### Sycophantic reflex
Form: praising the question or the reader before answering.
Examples: "Great question!" / "You're absolutely right" / "That's a really insightful point"
Instead: answer.
Status: no specimen found across three sampled sessions, including one predating the maintainer's style corrections. Plausibly a behavior of older models or other vendors.

### Hedge stack
Form: throat-clearing qualifiers that defer the claim.
Examples: "It's worth noting that" / "arguably" / "could potentially" / "in some ways"
Instead: commit to the claim or cut it.
Status: no clean specimen. Note that "It's worth noting that" now falls under form 11, labeled preamble, so part of this form is already covered.

### Colon-drama header
Form: "[Punchy phrase]: [explanatory subtitle]" as a title.
Examples: "The Real Problem: Why Your Onboarding Fails"
Instead: a plain descriptive header.
Status: no specimen. Likely a blog and article pattern rather than an assistant-conversation one.

### AI lexicon
Form: words statistically loud in AI text.
Examples: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, landscape, navigate, foster, unlock, journey, elevate
Instead: the plain verb or noun a colleague would use.
Status: no specimen. Several of these are stronger tells for other vendors' models than for Claude, which is what Deform targets.

### Audience straddle
Form: addressing every possible reader at once.
Examples: "Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned expert" / "for teams of any size"
Instead: write for the actual reader of this text.
Status: no specimen. Probably a marketing-copy pattern rather than a conversational one.
